## High level overview

This week, the Mithril team completed the managed Kubo node download mode and the support of the Kubo RPC API authentication in the client for IPFS.

The team kept working on the discovery of the facts of the Cardano node, the guessing of the Mithril signer and DMQ node parameters and the terminal user interface of the setup command, and the assessment of the impact of the client Kubo node acting as an IPFS peer.
They also worked on the prototypes of the certification adaptation in the Dijkstra era and of the Cardano database snapshot and restoration with the Leios database, and on the deployment of the `dev-dijkstranet` and `dev-musashinet` Mithril networks.

Finally, they updated the guild-operators scripts to use the on-chain protocol configuration, and worked on the preparation of the `2650` distribution, the migration of the npm packages publication to trusted publishing, and the upgrade to Cardano node `11.2`.
