## High level overview

This week, the Mithril team released the `2642` distribution. This version reads the protocol configuration from the Cardano chain in the signer and the aggregator, supports the Cardano node `11.1.3` and the DMQ node `0.7.2.0`, and removes the support for the `Gzip` compressed snapshot archives.
It also introduces a certificate chain cache in the client, support for compact SNARK certificates with a genesis-signed registry of the trusted circuit verification keys, and preliminary support for IPFS as a storage location of the immutable files (all unstable). It includes various bug fixes and improvements as well.

The team also published the immutable archives directory of the aggregator under an IPNS name, and selected a crate for the user interaction of the setup command.
They kept working on the deterministic resolution of the SNARK verification key collisions in the key registration, the progressive rollout strategy of the IPFS distribution, the new `mithril-setup` crate, and the findings of the M1.1 milestone audit.

Finally, they worked on the upgrade to Cardano node `11.2`, the renaming of `DeprecatedCommand` to `RemovedCommand` in the client CLI, the cleanup of the `mithril-test-lab` directory, and the investigation of the blocks and transactions verification failure on the `testing-preview` network.
