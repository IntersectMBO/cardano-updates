## Overview

### Summary

#### Ouroboros-Network

We released [`network-mux-0.12.0.0`, `ouroboros-network-1.3.0.0` and
`cardano-diffusion-1.3.0.0`][pr#5442], followed by the [`ouroboros-network`
2.0 release][pr#5463], which `dmq-node` has [already adopted][dmq-pr#80].
[`cardano-diffusion` 1.1.0.0][pr#5412] and [1.1.1.0][pr#5416] were released
earlier.

**Tx-submission.**  The [V2 tx-submission without a central decision
thread][pr#5375] was merged.  Since then we [fixed duplicate txids in the
outbound side][pr#5435], [fixed truncated duration counters][pr#5436] (waits
shorter than a millisecond were counted as zero), switched outbound
calculations to [`Int`][pr#5404], and made [`awaitSharedChange` an STM
action][pr#5399] so `dmq-node` can resolve races in a single transaction.
[`NodeToNodeV_16` with configurable tx-submission limits][pr#5446] was
merged.  [Bounding the work done during a sweep][pr#5458] (at most 1000
entries per STM transaction) is in progress.

**Network-mux.**  [Mux SDU header encoding/decoding][pr#5415] is now about 70%
faster and allocates over 90% less.  The [mux now forces the first 256 KiB of a
message in the sender's thread][pr#5456], so an unevaluated encoding waiting in
the egress buffer no longer retains whatever it refers to.  [Mux
counters][pr#5462] were added, enabling per-mini-protocol dashboards.  [Fixes to
mux timeouts][pr#5453] (cancelling timeouts when actions throw and keeping
timeout monitors cancellable under masking) are in review, and an [RTT-aware
mux][pr#5405] using echo-cookie RTT sampling is being prototyped.  Work has
started on a [mux egress scheduler][pr#5473], which serves bearers by tiers
and tracks egress counters and busy periods, alongside [batching changes in the
mux][pr#5474] (`withBatch`, `GrantAt`).

**Connection manager & inbound governor.**  Several race conditions were
fixed: [connection-manager / inbound-governor races][pr#5411] (including a
connection leak), a [race on new connections in the inbound governor][pr#5440]
(with a new IOSimPOR test), and the [inbound governor no longer blocks on a
stale `MuxFinished`][pr#5461].  Two small memory leaks were fixed, in the
[`KeepAlive` server][pr#5459] and in [`cardano-node`'s
`acceptAcceptor`][node-pr#6740].  The diffusion layer now [filters configured
or negotiated mini-protocols][pr#5460] before passing them to the mux.

**Peer selection.**  Peers whose [peer-share request failed are now backed
off][pr#5439] (default 1 hour), and the [peer-share time is only updated for
peers we are still connected to][pr#5452].  [Topology reload of local roots
given as IP addresses][pr#5455] is fixed (previously they waited for DNS-named
local roots, never updating when there were none).  [DNS TTL handling was
fixed][pr#5400], and [upstreamyness tracing][pr#5437] was added, also reducing
the likelihood of immediately re-promoting hot peers.  We also added a
[`Hashable` API][pr#5434], and work on [running diffusion on multiple network
interfaces][pr#5414] is in review.

**Ping.**  The [ping API for validating SRV records / registration
certificates][pr#5387] was merged, to be used by `cardano-cli`, together with
[output formatting improvements][pr#5409]; [further output
improvements][pr#5421] are in review.

**Window-stats.**  A new [`window-stats` library][pr#5384] provides efficient
rolling-window statistics backed by a finger tree — a first step towards
exposing connection metrics to applications.  It already powers the [Leios
percentile metrics][node-pr#6680] in `cardano-node`.  Follow-ups include
[API changes][pr#5423], [renaming][pr#5427] and an [insert performance
fix][pr#5464].

**Smol-world.**  We are building [`smol-world`][pr#5424], a discrete-event
simulator with a realistic transport model (loss, RFC 6298 RTO, CUBIC
congestion control per RFC 9438) on a small-world topology with per-node egress
contention, to study diffusion at scale.  [TCP model refinements][pr#5425] are
in review.

#### Leios

The [improved ΔQ analysis of Linear Leios EB diffusion][leios-pr#889] was
merged.  It fixes three bugs in the earlier model, sweeps over EB closure
sizes to determine practical limits, and analyses the sensitivity to mempool
fragmentation (1-hop vs. multi-hop closure fetch, `TxCache` miss rate).  We
also published the [incremental decoding benchmark results][leios-pr#1019] in
the Leios design document.  In `cardano-node` we added [CDFs of EB
announcement delays][node-pr#6639] (with a [follow-up fix][node-pr#6665]) and
[fixed the Praos CDF][node-pr#6707]; [replacing the CDFs with p90/p95/p99
percentiles][node-pr#6680] is in review.

We are also experimenting with a [whole-closure store-and-forward EB fetch
policy with bounded hedging][cons-pr#2513] in `ouroboros-consensus`: a peer
offers a closure only once it holds all of it, receivers request only the
missing transactions, and RTT-aware deadlines trigger hedged requests to
alternative suppliers when progress stalls.  In a 64-node `cardano-ignite`
experiment it reduced median time for a closure to reach all nodes from
5.6 s to 4.1 s, and cut redundant closure payload from 86 MB to 1 MB
(amplification 1.53× → 1.01×).

#### DMQ-Node

We released [`dmq-node-0.7.0.0`][dmq-pr#70] (`ouroboros-consensus-4.0`,
`ouroboros-network-1.2`, GHC-9.14 support) and
[`dmq-node-0.7.2.0`][dmq-pr#75], and [updated to `ouroboros-network-2.0` and
`ouroboros-consensus-5.2`][dmq-pr#80].  [Integration for Node release
11.1][dmq-pr#60] was completed.  Other changes include [peer-sharing timeouts
and an explicit CBOR decode error for local message notification][dmq-pr#69],
[changes to the signature outbound side][dmq-pr#74], [lowering the minimum
signature body size to 90 bytes][dmq-pr#65], an internal [`--min-sig-delay`
flag][dmq-pr#66], a [custom validation config fix][dmq-pr#67], and
[running Hydra CI on `aarch64-linux`][dmq-pr#72].

#### IOSim, io-classes & typed-protocols

[`io-classes-1.11` and `typed-protocols-1.2.2.0`][tp-pr#95] were released.
An [`io-sim` performance fix][io-sim-pr#265] (reverting eager evaluation of
`say`/`traceM`/`traceSTM` arguments) was merged, and [`ppSayTrace`][io-sim-pr#261]
was added.  A [fix for `timeout` raising a stale exception after it
returned][io-sim-pr#270] and a [`TimeDuration` type class][io-sim-pr#266] are in
review.  [`IOSimPOR` gained finer control over which threads take part in
schedule exploration][io-sim-pr#274] (`explorationRacyByLabel`, with labelled
`fork`/`async` variants); this is in review.
