## High level overview

This week, the Mithril team activated the `snark` feature by default, completed the prototype of the orchestrator of the setup command, and defined the migration of the existing immutable archives to the deterministic format.
They also supported the Kubo RPC API authentication in the aggregator, and published a DNSLink record of the immutable archives IPNS name in the DNS zones of the Mithril networks.

The team kept working on the managed Kubo node download mode and the Kubo RPC API authentication in the client, the discovery of the facts of the Cardano node for the setup command, the prototype of the certification adaptation in the Dijkstra era, and the deployment of a `dev-dijkstranet` Mithril network.

Finally, they simplified the serialization of the IVC proofs, removed the unused IPFS storage max setting from the infrastructure, and worked on the upgrade to Cardano node `11.2`.
