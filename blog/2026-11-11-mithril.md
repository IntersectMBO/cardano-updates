## High level overview

This week, the Mithril team stabilized the certificate chain cache in the client library and CLI, and reduced the size of the signature messages to fit within the limit of the DMQ node in `testing-preview`.

The team kept working on the activation of the `snark` feature by default, the prototype of the orchestrator of the setup command, and the migration of the existing immutable archives to the deterministic format.
They also started working on the discovery of the facts of the Cardano node for the setup command, and on the prototype of the certification adaptation in the Dijkstra era.

Finally, they upgraded Rust to version `1.100`, and worked on the upgrade to Cardano node `11.2` and on the update of the guild-operators scripts to use the on-chain protocol configuration.
